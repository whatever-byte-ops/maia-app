# Maia Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Maia is a reading library for stories you choose to add. This notice explains what the app stores and what happens when you use its optional online features.

## Data stored on your device

Maia stores your library, imported story text, reading progress, highlights, private notes, and preferences in local app storage. EPUB and saved HTML files are processed on your device and are not uploaded by the import process. Anyone with access to your unlocked device and Maia profile may be able to access this data.

When you use text-to-speech, Maia sends the text to your device's speech engine for playback. Maia does not send story text to its servers for speech synthesis. Your device or speech-engine provider may have separate privacy practices, particularly if you use a voice that requires network access.

## Optional account and synchronization

If account features are available and you choose to create an account, Supabase Authentication processes your email address and credentials. Maia does not store your password in your library.

Synchronization is optional and manual. When you select **Sync now**, Maia sends your library data—which may include imported story text, reading progress, notes, and highlights—to the Maia service associated with your account. Do not sync information you do not want stored with that service.

## Remote imports

When you request an AO3 or Wayback import, Maia sends the requested page URL to its configured import service. The service fetches the page and returns its contents to Maia for processing. Local EPUB and saved HTML imports do not use this service. Locked or inaccessible pages cannot be imported.

## Deletion

You can remove works and notes from your device in Maia. Removing local data does not delete a separately synced copy.

For account deletion, use **Settings → Reader Account Management → Manage Account → Delete account**. Account deletion removes the account and associated cloud-synced library data from the active Maia service. It does not erase data stored locally on your device; remove that separately. Deletion from service backups may take additional time.

If you cannot complete an account-deletion request in the app, contact [heatshifttechnologies@gmail.com](mailto:heatshifttechnologies@gmail.com) from the email address associated with the account.

## Analytics and advertising

Maia does not include advertising or analytics code.
