# Maia

Maia is a personal library for reading and organizing fanworks. Import stories, keep your place, add private notes and highlights, and listen with text-to-speech.

## Install on Android

Download the latest Android APK from the repository's [Releases page](https://github.com/whatever-byte-ops/maia/releases) and open it on your phone. Android may ask you to allow your browser or file manager to install apps. Only install APKs you trust.

## Use Maia

- **Add stories:** Import an EPUB or saved HTML file from your device. Remote AO3 and Wayback imports are available when the service is enabled and you are signed in.
- **Read:** Your place is saved as you read. Use the chapter menu to move between chapters.
- **Listen:** Open a story and tap **Listen**. Use the audio controls to pause, resume, stop, and change playback speed. While reading on Android, Maia uses your device's speech engine and highlights the spoken words. If speech does not start, open the audio controls and tap **Set up speech engine** to install or configure voice data.
- **Take notes:** Select text to highlight it or attach a private note. Work-wide notes are available from the work's details.
- **Keep your library private:** Stories, notes, highlights, reading progress, and preferences are stored on your device unless you choose to sync.
- **Sync between devices:** Sign in and choose **Settings → Synchronization & Backup → Sync now**. Sync is manual and uploads your library to the configured Maia service.

EPUB and saved HTML imports are processed on your device. Maia cannot import locked or otherwise inaccessible works; use a file you are authorized to access.
